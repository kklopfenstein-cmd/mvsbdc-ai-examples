How I use AI at our center

These are real examples from the Miami Valley SBDC, built with AI. Open them, try them, and borrow the ideas for your own center.

Web tool

STRIVE Business Plan Builder

A free, step-by-step business plan builder. Each step matches a STRIVE session, it does the financial projections, and it downloads the finished plan as a PDF or Word file. Work stays on the user’s own computer.

Open the builder
Branded client guide

How to Create a Capability Statement

A four-page MVSBDC Client Guide: the five parts of a capability statement, a step-by-step build, a checklist, and a full sample for a fictional veteran-owned company.

Webinar workbook

Recordkeeping for the Small Business

An eight-page MVSBDC participant workbook for a recordkeeping webinar. Each section follows IRS Publication 583, with fill-in tables, checklists, a quick quiz and an action plan.

View the workbook (PDF)

Instagram style sheet for the recordkeeping webinar

Nine “anti-AI” visual directions for webinar posts, from risograph print to stitched felt, each with a sample post. Includes brand color rules from the Ohio SBDC brand guide and the required SBA disclaimer.
