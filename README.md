# AI at the Miami Valley SBDC: Examples

Examples from Kim Klopfenstein's panel talk, "How I Use AI at Our Center," at the Ohio SBDC statewide conference (November 2026).

**View the examples:** https://kklopfenstein-cmd.github.io/mvsbdc-ai-examples/

## What's here

| Example | What it is |
|---|---|
| [STRIVE Business Plan Builder](https://kklopfenstein-cmd.github.io/sbdc-schema-tool/strive-business-plan-builder.html) | A free, step-by-step business plan builder that follows the STRIVE program, does the financial projections, and downloads the plan as a PDF or Word file |
| [How to Create a Capability Statement](examples/MVSBDC-Guide-Capability-Statement.pdf) | A four-page MVSBDC Client Guide with a full sample statement for a fictional company |
| [Recordkeeping for the Small Business workbook](examples/MVSBDC-Workbook-Recordkeeping.pdf) | An eight-page MVSBDC participant workbook from a recordkeeping webinar, following IRS Publication 583 |
| [Instagram style sheet](examples/Ohio-SBDC-Instagram-Style-Sheet.pdf) | Nine "anti-AI" visual directions for Instagram posts promoting the recordkeeping webinar, with brand color rules |
| [Panel slides](examples/How-I-Use-AI-Panel-Slides.pdf) | The presentation from the panel |

All examples were built with AI tools. Company names, numbers and contacts in the samples are fictional.
